Pundits constantly talk about "momentum shifting" during a match. While this sounds like a purely psychological concept, data scientists actually track momentum strictly as a mathematical time-series.

## Calculating Momentum

Momentum is calculated minute-by-minute using a weighted moving average of several factors:
- **Field Tilt**: Percentage of final-third passes.
- **Expected Threat (xT) generation**: The danger level of those passes.
- **Successful Defensive Actions**: Recoveries in the opponent's half.

When we plot this on a diverging area chart, we see the momentum pendulum swing wildly above and below the zero-axis between the home and away teams. 

Statistically, a goal rarely happens in isolation. It is almost always preceded by a sustained 3-to-5-minute peak of positive momentum (intense, localized pressure in the attacking third), which finally breaks the defensive probability model.
