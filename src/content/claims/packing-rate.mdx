A pass from one center-back to another is safe, but it rarely leads to a goal. To objectively measure which players are actually driving the offense forward, German analysts invented the concept of **Packing**.

## Taking Players Out of the Game

Packing evaluates a pass by counting the number of opposing players who are bypassed (or "packed") by the trajectory of the ball. If a midfielder threads a through-ball past the opponent's midfield line and into the feet of their striker, all the opponents caught behind the ball are mathematically taken out of the defensive equation.

In modern recruitment, data scientists prize midfielders and center-backs with high Packing Rates. A player who successfully packs 60 opponents per match is doing significantly more structural damage to the opposing defense than a player who makes 100 safe lateral passes that pack 0 opponents.
